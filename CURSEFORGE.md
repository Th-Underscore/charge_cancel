# Cancel Charge

Cancels an offhand item charge when the Attack key is pressed.

## What it does

While holding use (right-click) on an offhand item like a bow, press Attack (left-click) to instantly cancel the charge. Works with bows, crossbows, tridents, and any other chargeable item.

## Why?

I wanted to cancel bow charges in the offhand without switching back to the main hand or waiting for the draw to complete.

## Details

- Server-side compatible
- Multiplayer safe
- Forge 1.20.1
- Lightweight, no configuration needed
