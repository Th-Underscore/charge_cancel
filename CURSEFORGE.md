# Cancel Charge

Cancels an item charge when the Attack key (default) is pressed.

## What it does

While holding use (e.g. right-click) on any item like a bow, press Attack (e.g. left-click) to instantly cancel the charge. Works with bows, crossbows, tridents, and any other chargeable item, as well as most modded items (tested in [Otherworld [Dungeons & Dragons]](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/otherworld-dnd)). Compatible with rebound controls.

## Details

- Server-side compatible
- Multiplayer safe
- Forge 1.20.1
- Keybind-configurable ("Not Bound" automatically maps to Attack key)
